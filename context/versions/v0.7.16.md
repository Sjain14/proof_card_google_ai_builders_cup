# v0.7.16 · Replay mode

**Date:** 16 Oct 2026 IST · **Branch:** p7-replay-mode

## Shipped
- Timed clip transcription: `GeminiClipTranscriber.transcribe_timed` asks Gemini for one item per sentence with its MM:SS start (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/audio#refer-to-timestamps). `?timed=true` on the clip endpoint; untimed stays the default.
- `ClaimDoc.t_offset_s` stored on every live claim from a timed clip; `SessionView` claims expose it plus `matched_claim_id`.
- Live page: when the brief has a YouTube URL and timed live claims exist, "Replay with video" swaps the iframe for the IFrame Player API (https://developers.google.com/youtube/iframe_api_reference), polls `getCurrentTime()` every 250 ms and shows each card once the video reaches its second (seek / rewind stay in sync).
- Tests: `test_timed_clip_stores_video_offsets`, `test_clock_parsing`, vitest `replay`.

## Why
Demo: a recorded press-conference clip plays with cards appearing at the moment each claim is said, without needing live tab audio.
