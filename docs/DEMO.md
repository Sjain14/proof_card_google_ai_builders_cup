# Proof Card: how to use it + demo script

Site: https://aibuilderscup.web.app · Bot: https://t.me/ProofCardAgentBot

## How to use it (4 ways in)

1. **Before a speech (newsroom).** Open the site, "Continue as guest", paste the YouTube link (topic and speaker are optional), press Generate brief. Proof Card predicts the claims the speaker is likely to make, researches a fact bank (official data first: World Bank, IMF, government sources) and has a checked card ready for each before they speak.
2. **During a live stream.** Open the brief's live page, press listen and share the stream tab with tab audio on. A predicted claim shows its ready card instantly; a new claim is checked live. If Telegram is linked once, every live run posts by itself to Telegram: a start line, each FALSE / MISLEADING card as it is heard, and a summary at the end.
3. **A recorded clip.** On the live page, upload the clip's audio (or press "Check the sample clip"). Every sentence is checked; "Replay with video" pops each card at the second it was said.
4. **Telegram, for anyone.** Send the bot a claim, forward a message (up to 3 claims, one numbered verdict each, each a reply to that message), or send a screenshot of a forward. "X said '...'" is checked as a quote first: on record, twisted, or unproven. Forward a Proof Card image back to the bot to check it is the original (VERIFIED / ALTERED).

Every card carries the verdict (TRUE, MISLEADING, FALSE, UNVERIFIABLE), a one-line why, the exact source passage, a snapshot fingerprint and a QR, so anyone can verify it was not edited.

## Known limits (say them if asked)

- Quote records are found by web search: a quote said only in a video with no transcript online reads "unproven", not FALSE.
- Telegram: 40 checks per chat per day; a forward is split into at most 3 claims.
